Royal Jewel Block Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

In short

  • There is no account. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress, scores and settings are stored on your device.
  • The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how it is played, and Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (for example, to continue a game). These services collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before ads are personalised.

1. Who we are

Royal Jewel Block Puzzle (the "Game", package com.game.block.puzzle.royal) is published by Everyday App Utility ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your information, contact us at everydayapputility@gmail.com.

This policy explains what information the Game handles, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following on your device so you can continue where you left off:

  • your game in progress, best score and game statistics (games played, lines cleared, best combo);
  • your Adventure and seasonal progress and the themes you have earned;
  • your settings (sound, music, volume, vibration, effects) and whether you have completed the tutorial.

We do not receive this information. If you have turned on Android backup for your device, Android may include the Game's saved data in your Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data from the device.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses two services provided by Google. They collect information automatically when you play.

Google Firebase Analytics (to understand and improve the Game)

Firebase Analytics collects information about how the Game is used, such as:

  • gameplay events: games and levels started, finished, won, lost or left; scores and level numbers; revives; the screens you visit; tutorial progress; changes to settings; and whether ads loaded or were shown;
  • an app-instance identifier generated for this installation, and summary values such as how many games have been played, the best-score range and the current Adventure level;
  • device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, app version, approximate region (derived from your IP address) and session times.

We use this information to see which parts of the Game work well, to balance difficulty, to find problems and to plan improvements. We look at it in aggregate; we do not use it to identify you. See Firebase privacy and security.

Google AdMob (rewarded ads)

The Game offers optional rewarded ads, for example to continue a game. When ads are requested and shown, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and information about your interactions with ads. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, where you have consented (or where consent is not required), to personalise ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the Game shows Google's consent message before personalised ads are used. You can choose non-personalised ads there.

Information collected by Firebase Analytics and AdMob is processed by Google LLC and its affiliates as our service provider or, for some advertising purposes, as an independent controller under Google's own terms. We do not share information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

6. International transfers

Google may process information in the United States and other countries. Google relies on appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, for transfers out of the EEA and the UK.

7. How long information is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the Game. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for a limited period set in our Google Analytics settings and is then deleted automatically. Advertising data is kept by Google according to its own retention policies.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Ads. Rewarded ads are always optional. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Consent. Where Google's consent message applies, you can change your choice by clearing the Game's data in Android settings and opening the Game again.
  • Delete your data. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes everything stored on the device and starts a new app-instance identifier. You can also ask us to delete analytics data linked to your installation.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your information, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because the Game has no accounts, we may need some details from you (such as the device and the approximate date you played) to find the information. Contact us at everydayapputility@gmail.com.

9. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for consent to data processing in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Information sent to Google's services travels over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use reputable providers and collect only what the Game needs.

11. Permissions

The Game asks for internet and network-state access (to load ads and send analytics) and vibration (for haptic feedback). It does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game or the law changes. We will post the new version here and change the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will let you know in the Game.
